# Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Circuit Timer: Interval HIIT ("the app") is published by Andrey Krementsov, an individual developer ("I"). This policy explains what the app does with your data. In short: the app sends nothing to me. Your workouts stay on your devices and, if you choose, in your own iCloud and Apple Health.

## What I receive

Nothing from the app. The app has no accounts, servers, analytics, advertising or tracking, and no third-party service of mine receives any data from it.

If you write to me by email, I receive what you choose to send. I keep it in my mailbox only to answer you and delete it when you ask.

## On your device

The app keeps on your iPhone:

- your workouts, their stages and their order;
- your settings, such as sound, vibration and spoken stage names;
- the state of a running workout, so that it can continue after the app is closed.

While a workout runs, the app schedules notifications on your device and shows a Live Activity on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island. Spoken stage names are produced on the device.

Deleting the app removes all of this from your iPhone.

## iCloud

If you buy the full version and turn on iCloud sync, the app stores your workouts, with their names and stages, in your private iCloud database. Only your Apple Account can read it; I have no access to it. If iCloud is unavailable, your workouts stay only on the device.

A workout you delete in the app is removed from iCloud when the deletion syncs. Turning iCloud sync off takes effect at the next launch and does not delete what iCloud already holds. To delete it, delete the workouts while sync is on, or remove the app's data in iOS Settings under your name › iCloud › Manage Storage, where iOS offers it.

## Apple Health

On iOS 26 and later, and only if you allow it, the app saves the workouts its timer runs to Apple Health as high-intensity interval training, from their start until they end or are closed, without their pauses. The timer may save none, for example when Health does not start its workout session. The app never reads your Health data.

Health keeps these workouts on your device and syncs them only under your own Health and iCloud settings. You can delete them in the Health app, and turn off the app's access in the Health app under your profile › Apps › Circuit Timer.

## Purchases

The full version is sold through the App Store. Apple handles the payment; the app only learns from the App Store whether the full version is yours.

## Apple Watch

The watch app shows your workouts and the running workout and sends your commands to the iPhone. This passes only between your own iPhone and Apple Watch.

## Apple services

iCloud, Apple Health, the App Store and the link to Apple Watch are Apple's services and follow [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Children

The app collects no personal data from anyone, children included.
